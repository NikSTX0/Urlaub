Palau is one of the smallest countries in the world, with a population of only 18,000 and a total area of only 465 square kilometers.
Even so, it's one of the most beautiful countries in the world, and the Rock Islands are listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
Two of the most popular bucket list things to do in Palau are an island hopping tour of the Rock Islands by boat, or a scenic flight over the Rock Islands. Another popular activity is scuba diving because of the area's world class coral reefs.
Palau is located in the Micronesia region of the western Pacific Ocean, neighbored by Guam, Papua, and the Philippines.
The only international airport in Palau is in Koror (ROR). There are direct flights from Brisbane, Guam, Manila, Seoul, Taipei and Tokyo.
Palau is a very safe country. The climate is tropical, hot and humid year round. There is a strict ban on chemical sunscreen to protect the coral reefs - only mineral sunscreen is allowed.
