New Zealand is a wonderful country for a road trip, especially on the mostly rural South Island, where they have more sheep than people.
The North Island has great beaches, volcanoes, and green farmlands, while the South Island has glaciers, fjords, rain forests, and arguably the best road tripping conditions and scenery.
New Zealand is where the entire Lord of the Rings movie series was filmed, and they even kept some of the movie sets.
The main airport in New Zealand is Auckland (AKL) on the North Island. On the South Island, the biggest airport is Christchurch (CHC).
New Zealand is one of the safest countries in the world, with a low violent crime rate of 0.7 per 100k inhabitants. The climate is temperate and mild with summer from December to February and winter from June to August.
New Zealand is not a cheap country. You can expect to pay over 2 New Zealand Dollars per liter for gas. Eating out costs upwards of 20 NZD for a casual meal.
