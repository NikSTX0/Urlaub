Zhangjiajie National Forest Park in China is one of the most unique places in the world, and it inspired the floating mountains in James Cameron's Avatar movie.
It's a big park that's pretty spread out, so at least 2 full days are recommended to do it justice, not counting other nearby attractions like Tianmen Mountain.
There are three main base options: Zhangjiajie Town with the airport and train station 30 kilometers south of the park; Wulingyuan Town right outside the east entrance with plenty of hotels and restaurants; and YOLO Resort directly inside the park, ideal for watching sunrise and sunset.
The park features massive sandstone pillar formations rising dramatically from the forest floor, with glass-bottomed walkways and cable cars offering breathtaking views.
