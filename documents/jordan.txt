The kingdom of Jordan is emerging as one of the most popular countries to visit in the Middle East.
The highlight of any trip to Jordan is Petra, the hidden city in the rock - an incredible ancient town carved into the rocks in southern Jordan, explored by foot or on the back of a camel.
Another popular attraction is the desert valley of Wadi Rum, where you can take a jeep tour around the orange sand dunes and huge granite mountains.
The main international airport in Jordan is in Amman (AMM). There's also an airport in Aqaba (AQJ) if you want to fly in closer to Petra.
Jordan is a relatively safe place to travel, with a very low violent crime rate of 1.6 per 100k inhabitants. Jordanian culture is conservative, so clothing in public should be modest.
The climate of Jordan is generally arid, with hot summers and cool, short winters. The coolest months are from November to March. I went in October, and the heat was not an issue at all.
