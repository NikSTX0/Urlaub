The Middle Eastern country of Oman has some incredible sights that range from historic castles to desert canyons and rocky mountains.
The highlight of a trip to Oman is the Nakhal Fort and the Grand Canyon of Oman, both of which can be visited easily on day trips from the capital city of Muscat.
Oman is known for its warm hospitality, stunning desert landscapes, and well-preserved ancient forts. The capital Muscat offers a blend of modern amenities and traditional Arabian culture.
The best time to visit Oman is from October to April when temperatures are more comfortable. Summer months can be extremely hot, especially in the interior desert regions.
