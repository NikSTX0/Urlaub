India is a big culture shock with amazing architecture and natural sights, including the Taj Mahal in Agra and snow capped mountains in the Himalayas.
The Golden Triangle of India is a popular tourist circuit through Delhi, Agra and Jaipur. The main draw is the incredible white marble Taj Mahal, a masterpiece on every traveler's bucket list.
Other worthwhile attractions include Humayun's Tomb in Delhi and the Hawa Mahal in Jaipur.
Main airports are New Delhi (DEL) and Jaipur (JAI). India is a relatively safe travel destination with a homicide rate of 3.2 per 100k inhabitants. Women traveling alone may want to join a shared tour. Road safety can be an issue due to chaotic traffic; taking the train is safer for long distances.
India is a very budget friendly place to travel, with hostels from 250 Rupees and meals costing about 80 to 400 Rupees. Transportation is generally by tuk-tuk, car, or train.
