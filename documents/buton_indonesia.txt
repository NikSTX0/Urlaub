I think Buton is probably one of the most interesting places to visit in Indonesia, along with its neighboring island Muna.
We just spent 10 days in Buton and Muna, and still didn't see everything. This island in Sulawesi has great waterfalls, caves, and exotic beaches, and none of the crowds of foreign tourists you might see in Bali or other parts of Indonesia.
Buton is also home to a group of blue-eyed people from the Kaimbulawa tribe, famous for their amazing eyes.
Buton island is located off the coast of southeast Sulawesi. The best way to get there is by flying to the main city of Baubau (BUW), or you can take a ferry from one of the neighboring places, such as Muna, Kendari, or Wakatobi.
If you're flying from Bali or Jakarta, you'll need to transit in Makassar (UPG) on the way to Buton. The flight from Makassar to Baubau takes a little over 1 hour. The other main way to get to Buton is the fast ferry from Kendari to Baubau, which departs twice daily and takes 6 hours.
