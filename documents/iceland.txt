Iceland is the land of fire and ice, a country of extreme landscapes and incredible beauty where you can walk on a glacier and see a volcano in the same day.
One of the main reasons tourists come to Iceland is to see the waterfalls - this country has some of the best waterfalls in the world, with over 10,000 waterfalls formed by rugged mountains and melting glaciers.
The main airport in Iceland is in Keflavik (KEF), about 50 kilometers from the capital Reykjavik, with direct flights from Canada, Europe, and the United States.
Iceland is a great place for solo and female travelers, with an extremely low violent crime rate of 0.3 per 100k inhabitants - one of the safest countries in the world.
Iceland is tricky to visit on a budget. Hostels start from 3,000 Krona and a simple restaurant meal can cost 3,000 Krona. One way to save is to rent a camper van - Iceland has lots of campgrounds spread across the country.
