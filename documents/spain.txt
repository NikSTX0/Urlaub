Spain has a little bit of everything: mountains, city life, architecture, cuisine, coastlines, culture, history, and more.
Northern Spain is known for the cathedrals of Barcelona, and you can get hiking and scenery in the mountains at Montserrat National Park.
The three main airports in Spain are Madrid (MAD), Barcelona (BCN) and Palma de Mallorca (PMI). Spain is generally a safe place to travel with a very low violent crime rate of 0.6 per 100k inhabitants. Pickpocketing is common in Barcelona so practice situational awareness.
The climate of Spain is mostly continental and Mediterranean - coastal parts have hot, dry summers and mild winters. The best time to visit is spring or fall.
Hostels start from 10 Euros and private hotels from 40 Euros. Meals are about 5 to 10 Euros. Transportation is generally by bus or train.
